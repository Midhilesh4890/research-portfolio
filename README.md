## Publications

### 1. Uncertainty-Aware Molecular Property Prediction Using Heterogeneous Molecular Graph Neural Networks (2025)
**Momidi, M., Chauhan, P. S., Komaraneni, A., Ghattamaneni, S. P., Upreti, K., & Kumar, N.** *Proceedings of International Conference on Generative AI, Cryptography and Predictive Analytics*, Springer, pp. 243–254.

[Publication / DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-981-97-9132-3_16)

This research introduces an Uncertainty-Aware Heterogeneous Graph Neural Network (UA-HGNN) for molecular property prediction by representing complex many-body interactions. It combines neural message passing, attention, and uncertainty estimation, with evaluation on the QM9 dataset.

### 2. Advanced Sentiment Analysis: From Lexicon-Enhanced BERT to Dimensionality Reduction Using NLP (2024)
**Kumar, V., Chauhan, P. S., Lella, J. A., Momidi, M., Gupta, P., & Upreti, K.** *International Conference on Emerging Trends in Networks and Computer Communications (ETNCC)*, IEEE, pp. 441–446.

[Publication / DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/ETNCC63262.2024.10767483)

This paper explores sentiment analysis on noisy social-media text using BERT representations, CNNs, N-grams, sentiment lexicons, and dimensionality reduction. It addresses domain variation, multilingual data, and code-mixed language.

### 3. Towards Resilient Darknet Security: Integrating Transfer Learning and Dynamic Ensemble Techniques (2024)
**Biradar, P., Prasad, P., Momidil, M., Polavarapu, S., Kumar, V., & Mittal, S.** *International Conference on Intelligent Systems for Cybersecurity (ISCS)*, IEEE.

[Publication / DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/ISCS61804.2024.10581038)

This research explores detecting and classifying darknet network traffic using transfer learning and dynamic ensemble models. It also considers explainable AI to make predictions more interpretable as traffic patterns change.

### 4. Unified Spatio-Temporal Attention Models for Advanced Human Action Recognition & Detection (2024)
**Momidi, M., Jadhav, P., Shrey, Patel, A., & Modi, A.** *International Journal for Research in Applied Science and Engineering Technology (IJRASET).*

[Publication / DOI](https://doi.org/10.22214/IJRASET.2024.58130)

This work examines two approaches to human action recognition: recurrent modeling of skeletal joints and a spatio-temporal attention network over video features. The focus is on identifying the motion patterns, frames, and channels most useful for recognition.

### 5. Towards Innovative Neural Network Paradigms: Enhanced EEG Emotion Recognition Through Hybrid STANN-3DCANN Deep Architectures (2024)
**Geethanjali, P., Metun, Biswas, D., Momidi, M., & Sarika, D. N.** *International Journal of Creative Research Thoughts (IJCRT)*, 12(1), a859–a871.

[Full-text PDF](https://ijcrt.org/papers/IJCRT2401111.pdf)

This paper studies EEG-based emotion recognition using spatio-temporal and convolutional attention networks. The approaches are evaluated on multiple emotion-recognition datasets, including DEAP, DREAMER, EEWD, and SEED.
